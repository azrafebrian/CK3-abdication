# Abdication | Continue as Heir

Adds Abdicate the Throne decision, allowing you to pass your titles to your primary heir and play as them. Made to blend in seamlessly with the game and balanced with character modifiers.

## Installation and Use

1. Go To https://github.com/azrafebrian/CK3-abdication/releases and find the latest version, and **download**.
2. Extract the downloaded .zip file.
3. Copy or move halfsade_abdicate folder and halfsade_abdicate.mod into \Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings III\mod
4. Open the game launcher and add mod to a playset, and enable the mod. 
5. Play game with selected playset.

## About This Mod

### Features

1. Abdicate the Throne decision, pass your titles to your primary heir. Located in personal decision group.
2. Character and opinion modifiers, along with realistic requirements to balance the mechanic.
3. Confirmation to continue abdicate or cancel, along with 15 days preparation time.

### Requirements

1. Is a ruler
2. Have a primary heir
3. Doesn't have traits like Ambitious, Conqueror, or Greedy
4. Your Primary Heir doesn't have the trait Incapable
5. Your Primary Heir is not imprisoned, at war against you, and your rival
6. Your Primary Heir is not your *predecessor

*: former ruler that abdicates via this decision. a former ruler who is a primary heir may still inherit your titles in case your character dies.

### Effects

1. All your titles passed to your heir, and you play as them.
2. Dynamic modifiers applied to you for a certain time.

### Limitations

1. Cooldown for 5 years if abdication is cancelled.
2. Traits like Ambitious, Conqueror, and Greedy will disable decision.
3. AI will not be able to do this decision.
